We are a small startup company creating online learning solutions for calculus.  We are looking to contract a content creator to write material explaining the following three numerical methods of integration

* the midpoint rule
* the trapezoid rule
* Simpson's rule

To complete this commission, your submission should be a 3-5 page report explaining in detail each of these methods, including explicit examples, intended for the consumption of freshman in a first-year calculus course.  The explanations should highlight the similarities and differences between the methods.
